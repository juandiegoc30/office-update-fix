01 / The case

The purchase was still there.
Office asked to activate.

A relative owned Office Home & Business 2021 on an Apple Silicon Mac. The purchase still appeared in their Microsoft account, but a licensing error followed an update. Removing the apps and downloading them again from the account did not resolve it.

A Reddit discussion described a similar case and suggested returning to an earlier release. This project turns that experience into an assisted, documented process anyone can inspect. The exact cause and recovery outcome on the original Mac remain unconfirmed.

WHAT WE KNOW

Microsoft lists the October 6, 2026 installer, version 16.113.4. It is our recovery candidate, not a promise of activation or a certified final compatible release.

02 / Before you start

Back up first. Make changes second.

  • An Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later, with administrator access. The original Mac may run Tahoe; its exact version is unconfirmed.
  • A legitimate Office 2021 license linked to your Microsoft account. Record the error and your current Word version.
  • A recent, recoverable external backup including local Outlook data. Quit Office before backing up and check what is included. Also export an .olm archive if your Outlook version offers it.
  • Free space equal to your local data plus 25 GiB. The download is about 2.9 GB. Quit Office, log out other users and finish ongoing installations.

03 / Step by step

From download to verification.

1Download and open

Extract the ZIP and open Office-Update-Fix.command. Choose English and start with Diagnose. If macOS blocks it, inspect the code and use Terminal instead; do not disable Gatekeeper.

2Or use Terminal

Type cd followed by a space, drag the extracted folder into Terminal and press Return. Run diagnostics first:

bash scripts/office-update-fix.sh diagnose en

3Prepare recovery

The assistant copies standard Outlook data locations, downloads the official installer and checks its signature. After another confirmation, it saves the previous apps, installs the full suite without Teams and removes AutoUpdate before and after installation.

bash scripts/office-update-fix.sh recover en

4Check your license and your data

Open Word and sign in with the account that purchased Office 2021. Verify editing works. Then check Outlook mail, local folders, contacts and calendars. Stop if anything is missing or an unexpected migration is requested.

Only need to remove AutoUpdate?

This command removes the updater without reinstalling Office. It asks for confirmation and retains a backup of its components.

bash scripts/office-update-fix.sh remove-autoupdate en

04 / Outlook & recovery

Preserved data is not a compatibility guarantee.

The assistant does not delete profiles, licenses or Keychain. It keeps an extra local copy, but an older Outlook may not read an updated profile. Custom profile locations and other users need their own backup.

Read how to recover apps and data ↗

What removing AutoUpdate means

It affects every app relying on that updater. It does not block the App Store, other tools or manual installation. A Microsoft installer can bring it back; run diagnostics afterward. This is not a permanent guarantee.

Office 2021 support ended October 13, 2026. Staying on an old version means missing future security fixes. The recovery guide explains how to re-enable the updater.
